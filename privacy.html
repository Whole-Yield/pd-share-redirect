Privacy and Terms: PD reels loop

PD reels loop is an internal tool run by Perfumed Decay. It is not offered to the public and has no users other than Perfumed Decay itself.

What it does. It posts short clips of the Perfumed Decay podcast to Perfumed Decay's own YouTube channel and Instagram account, and reads those accounts' own view and engagement numbers.

What data it uses. Only data belonging to Perfumed Decay's own accounts: our videos, our channel and account details, and our analytics. It does not collect or store information about anyone else and does not sign anyone else in. We never sell, license, or share this data.

Google. PD reels loop's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Meta. Data received from Instagram is used only to publish our own posts and read our own statistics, and is deleted when no longer needed or on request.

Storage and removal. Access keys are stored only on Perfumed Decay's own computer. The account owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google) or in Instagram under Settings, Apps and websites. To request deletion of any data, email perfume.d.ecay@gmail.com.

Terms. The tool is provided as-is for Perfumed Decay's own use. There is nothing to sign up for or buy.
