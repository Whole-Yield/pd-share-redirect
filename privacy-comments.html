Privacy and Terms: PD Comment Replies

PD Comment Replies is an internal tool run by Perfumed Decay. It is not offered to the public and has no users other than Perfumed Decay itself.

What it does. It reads the public comments people leave on Perfumed Decay's own YouTube videos and posts replies from Perfumed Decay's channel. A person at Perfumed Decay approves each day's replies before anything is posted.

What data it uses. The public text of those comments and the commenter's public YouTube display name, plus our own channel's details. We keep them only on Perfumed Decay's own computer, only to draft, approve and track our replies. We never sell, license, or share this data, except that comment text is processed by an AI service provider for the sole purpose of drafting a reply.

Google. PD Comment Replies' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and removal. Access keys are stored only on Perfumed Decay's own computer. The account owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. If you commented on one of our videos and want your comment removed from our records, email perfume.d.ecay@gmail.com.

Terms. The tool is provided as-is for Perfumed Decay's own use. There is nothing to sign up for or buy.
